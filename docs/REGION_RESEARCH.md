# Project Demeter — Regional Evidence Baseline

**Research refresh:** 2026-08-19  
**Purpose:** Seed the interactive regional atlas with dated evidence while keeping sourced facts separate from planning judgments.

## Evidence rules

- **Sourced fact** — current or durable figure from an authoritative source. Keep the source URL and observation date.
- **Volatile signal** — listing, schedule, market or policy state that can change. Always date it.
- **Planning judgment** — Demeter's comparative interpretation. Never present as an external fact.
- **Parcel question** — cannot be answered regionally; requires property-level due diligence.

## Southwest Ontario

### Sourced facts

- Environment and Climate Change Canada 1991–2020 climate normals for London show a **January daily mean of −5.4°C**.
- VIA Rail currently shows **London → Toronto average duration 2h33m**, **185 km**, and **41 departures per week**.
- Farm Credit Canada reported average **Ontario farmland values increased 2.2% in 2025**.

### Planning judgment

Demeter currently treats Southwest Ontario as the **value + Toronto-access baseline**. It loses heavily on winter mildness versus coastal/southwestern BC, but existing rural housing stock, agricultural land, towns, hospitals and rail access can make the overall system more practical for a Toronto-linked remote worker.

### Search lanes

- Norfolk County / Simcoe / Waterford
- Tillsonburg / Norwich and adjacent Oxford–Norfolk areas
- Elgin / St. Thomas / Aylmer
- Middlesex / London outskirts / Strathroy–Caradoc

### Parcel questions

Legal access; zoning; well record/yield; potability; septic age/condition and reserve area; drainage; flood/conservation constraints; internet; winter road maintenance; house envelope; outbuilding condition; usable sunny land.

## Fraser Valley, British Columbia

### Sourced facts

- ECCC 1991–2020 climate normals for Abbotsford show a **January daily mean of 3.7°C**.
- TransLink's West Coast Express runs between Mission City and downtown Vancouver on weekdays. The published morning schedule shows Mission City **05:25 → Waterfront 06:40**, about **75 minutes**; service is direction- and rush-hour-specific rather than all-day rapid transit.
- BC's Agricultural Land Reserve gives priority to agriculture. Housing, subdivision, soil/fill activity and non-farm uses can be restricted, and local government rules continue to apply.

### Volatile market signal

- A current REW listing used only as a scale check showed **10 acres at 8573 S River Road, Mission listed at $1,548,800**, observed in August 2026. This is **not** a regional valuation or price forecast.

### Planning judgment

Demeter treats the Fraser Valley as the **climate + Vancouver-access contender**. The mildness advantage is large enough to matter to day-to-day homestead work, but land cost and ALR/local-use constraints mean parcel selection carries more economic and regulatory pressure.

### Search lanes

- Mission rural fringe / Hatzic / Deroche direction
- Chilliwack rural areas
- Agassiz / Harrison agricultural fringe

### Parcel questions

ALR status; local zoning; floodplain; riparian/environmental setbacks; soil/fill history; water source; wastewater approval; road access; wildfire exposure; exact WCE usefulness; travel time to hospital/grocery; price premium for usable rather than merely scenic acreage.

## Central / South Vancouver Island

### Sourced facts

- ECCC 1991–2020 normals for Victoria Airport show a **January daily mean of 4.6°C**. Demeter uses this only as a **maritime-climate proxy**, not as a claim that Cowichan or Nanaimo-area parcels have identical conditions.

### Planning judgment

Demeter treats Vancouver Island as the **lifestyle + fully-remote wildcard**. It has the strongest current mildness/privacy combination in the model, but city access is structurally different: ferry/air dependence and exact local hospital/service access matter more than a single commute number.

### Search lanes

- Cowichan Valley
- Cedar / south Nanaimo hinterland
- Other central-island acreage only after water, wildfire and service-access screening

### Parcel questions

Water availability and summer security; well records; wildfire/interface exposure; insurance; slope; forest management; hospital/grocery access; ferry/airport friction; septic; zoning; usable sunny land; whether geographic separation remains attractive after repeated travel.

### Source

- ECCC Victoria Airport normals: https://climate.weather.gc.ca/climate_normals/results_1991_2020_e.html?climate_id=1018620

## Cross-region system notes

### Water

Ontario places responsibility on private well owners for maintaining compliant wells and recommends/mandates licensed well contractors for regulated work. Demeter therefore models **owner literacy + professional well work**, rather than DIY well construction.

### Wastewater

Rural septic is owner-responsibility infrastructure with high replacement consequences. Acquisition should privilege a known, inspectable system and enough land for a future replacement solution over cosmetic house quality.

### Power

Natural Resources Canada distinguishes grid-connected and off-grid photovoltaic systems; off-grid PV normally requires storage. Demeter's default is therefore **grid-optional resilience**—keep a useful grid connection, then add solar/battery/generator capability where it improves resilience or economics.

Source: https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/solar-photovoltaic-energy-buildings
